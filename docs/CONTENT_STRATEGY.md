# Content Strategy

This document describes the content goals, structure, and editorial approach for CodeWithIshant v2.

## Mission

Enable developers to learn through structured topics, practical notes, searchable cheatsheets, blogs, and interactive visualizations.

## Target Content Types

- **Topics**: learning categories that organize notes and related content.
- **Notes**: tutorial-style content with examples and explanations.
- **Cheatsheets**: concise reference guides for quick learning.
- **Blogs**: longer narrative posts and announcements.
- **Visualizer pages**: interactive algorithm walkthroughs.

## Content Principles

- **Accuracy**: content should be technically correct and up to date.
- **Clarity**: use plain language and clear examples.
- **Structure**: organize content into logical sections with headings.
- **Discoverability**: use slugs, tags, and summaries to make content easy to find.
- **Reusability**: allow content to be reused across topics and collections.

## Content Taxonomy

- Top-level topics represent major learning areas.
- Notes belong to a topic and may include examples, code, and diagrams.
- Cheatsheets are referenceable summaries of key concepts.
- Blogs capture announcements, deep dives, or perspective pieces.

## Editorial Workflow

1. Create or update content in the admin UI.
2. Use descriptive titles, slugs, and summaries.
3. Preview before publishing to ensure formatting and correctness.
4. Keep content evergreen and periodically review older notes.

## Writing Guidelines

- Begin with a clear summary or introduction.
- Use code blocks for examples and highlight important keywords.
- Include a short summary or takeaway section.
- Keep paragraphs short and scannable.

## SEO and Metadata

- Use descriptive page titles and social-friendly metadata.
- Provide meaningful `title` and `description` metadata for public pages.
- Use topic slugs that reflect the content in a readable way.

## Content Maintenance

- Regularly audit outdated content and remove or revise obsolete examples.
- Track content gaps: missing topics, incomplete notes, or stale references.
- Use analytics or user feedback to prioritize content updates.

## Success Metrics

- Content coverage across the primary topic areas.
- Ease of discovery for notes and reference material.
- Quality of content presentation and readability.
- Feedback from learners and maintainers.
